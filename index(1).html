    How to test

    Cursor follows your index fingertip when the "Cursor" toggle is on. Turn it off any time and tracking continues without it.

    Pinch (touch thumb and index tip together) while the cursor is over SCAN or ALERT to click it — like a real tap.

    Make a fist anywhere in frame to toggle the flashlight indicator (a real flashlight too, on the back camera, if your browser supports it).

    Uses MediaPipe Hands, loaded from a CDN, for real 21-point hand tracking — the first start may take a few seconds while it loads. If it fails to load, the camera still works, just without tracking. All processing happens on your phone; no video leaves the device.
